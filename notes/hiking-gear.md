# Hiking Gear

Packing light makes short trails feel easier and long climbs feel less crowded. A small checklist keeps the basics from getting left on the kitchen table.

## Trail Basics

Bring sturdy shoes, a full water bottle, and a light shell for sudden weather. Even simple routes feel better when the essentials are easy to reach.

An extra snack can turn a rushed walk into a relaxed afternoon. It also helps to keep a map downloaded before leaving town.
