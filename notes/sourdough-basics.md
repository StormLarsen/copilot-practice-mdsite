# Sourdough Basics

The starter does most of the hard work, so the routine matters more than fancy tools. A calm pace makes the process feel less intimidating.

Mix flour and water until the dough looks shaggy, then let it rest before folding. That short pause gives the texture a **softer**, more elastic feel.

One bold step is to trust the dough when it still looks a little rough. It usually smooths out later with time and gentle handling.
